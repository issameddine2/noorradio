Privacy Policy

Noor Radio · Last updated October 25, 2026

Noor Radio (“the app”) is developed by Issameddine Ajmi (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information I collect

I do not collect, store or receive any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to me.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your iPhone only: your favorite stations, the stations you chose to hide, and a temporary copy of the station list so the app opens faster. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Third-party services

To show stations and play audio, the app connects to servers that I do not control:

  • The Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info), which provides the list of stations.
  • The servers of the individual radio stations, which provide the audio streams and station logos.

When the app connects to these servers, they can see your IP address and technical details such as your device type, as with any website or streaming service. Their own privacy policies apply. I do not receive this information.

Reporting a station

If you use “Report station”, the app opens your email app with a pre-filled message to me. I receive only what you choose to send, including your email address, and I use it only to review the station and reply to you. I delete such emails when the matter is resolved.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
